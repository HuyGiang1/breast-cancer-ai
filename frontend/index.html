Safety & limitations

Research evidence is not clinical validation.

  • Research / Educational Prototype; not a diagnostic medical device.
  • No external validation and no validated paired multimodal study.
  • CBIS grouping is not verified patient-level.
  • Clinical decisions require clinician assessment and pathology when appropriate.
